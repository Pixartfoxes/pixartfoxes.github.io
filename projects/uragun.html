Uragun

Top-down action twin-stick shooter, Kool2Play

A top-down action twin-stick shooter where you pilot a combat mech through several biomes, from neon-lit Hong Kong rooftops to desert cities.

Developer

Engine

My role

Project duration

My role

Game & Level Designer

  • Core gameplay

    I defined the core gameplay loop, mech controls and foundational combat mechanics.

  • Combat and balance

    I designed high-intensity combat encounters, enemy wave pacing and difficulty curves across the campaign.

  • Levels

    I architected levels across all of the game’s biomes, taking them from early layout blockouts to final technical setup.

  • Boss

    I created and led the first version of the main boss encounter, the Sand Guardian (codename: Centipede): its vanilla design and core mechanics, from initial concept to a working implementation that the team later built on.
